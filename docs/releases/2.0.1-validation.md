## Audio display fix

The old single-file view showed the last unfinished task and duplicated its child link in the full trace. Batch traces listed every child without showing its execution state. A shared task view now lists every active window with its number, state and waiting time, and folds the remaining tasks into history with their result, duration and failure note. Each task appears once. Batch members also show completed segment counts and avoid repeating the queued label.

The regression fixture reproduces the reported five-file batch with sixteen proofreading tasks: three active, twelve complete and one failed. The new assertion failed before the UI change and passed afterward. It verifies three active rows, thirteen historical rows, unique labelled child links, failure details and English localization. The existing single-file test now checks that its active child is not repeated in history.
