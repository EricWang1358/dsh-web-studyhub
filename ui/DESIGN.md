# Study panel design (1.0)

## Daily learning action hub

Mode: directed by the existing ink-on-desk system. Returning learners should see what to do next and their remaining daily allowance within five seconds. The primary object is the next action, not a dashboard of tasks or a negotiation form.

Thesis: a quiet daily briefing with one large action title and one cinnabar launch control. A thin progress rule and modest time labels orient the learner; the full plan sits behind a native disclosure. An unaccepted AI proposal temporarily takes this focal position so consent stays explicit. AI feedback and regular pace are separate, on-demand editors.

Taste constraints: no equal-weight task cards, no always-visible settings form, no invented precision for remaining task time. In-progress actions come first. Completed history stays accessible for time recording. Learning pages show a compact connection to today's action without a second launch button. Keep the established fonts, colour tokens and keyboard-visible controls; introduce no animation or external assets.

Subtraction: remove the persistent feedback form, the repeated row reasons and launch controls from context strips, the nested proposal box, and the decorative top rule. Preserve explanations where they support a decision: the next action, proposal review and optional editors. The action title owns display scale; the briefing heading stays small. Keep the launch adjacent to its duration rather than across an empty row.

The whole panel follows one idea: **the card is the only physical object; everything else is type on a desk.** Every new surface should either strengthen that idea or solve a named product problem.

## Thesis

- Intent: "I know exactly what to do today" — calm, confident, generous (大气).
- Composition: one focal object per page, placed asymmetrically. Library: today's paper card stack. Review: the question as an index card. Stats: the streak numeral (the active days while the streak is broken). Exam: the exam sheet. Skeleton: the learning axis.
- Secondary information is typographic lines under hairlines, never a grid of equal boxes.
- Type: `--font-ui` / `--font-display` start with the bundled "StudyHub Inter" for digits and Latin letters only (`ui/fonts/`: one variable woff2 with wght and opsz, OFL; `installInterFace` loads it once from a lazy chunk as a `data:` face; its `unicode-range` excludes every CJK block), then the system stack, so Chinese stays in the system face. A typed custom font comes before it and wins. Large light numerals for counts (opsz gives them the display cut); `tabular-nums` for counters; bold display headings; small muted labels. Inter's digits are wider than YaHei's: a fixed-width column that holds numbers is sized for the widest real value (`tests/inter-font.test.mjs`, `tests/wave5c-estimate.test.mjs`).
- Icons: one registry, `<Icon name>` (`ui/components/Icon.jsx`), drawn in **Phosphor Light** (MIT; fill-based, 256 grid, `currentColor`). `scripts/icons/build-icons.mjs` maps our names to Phosphor names and writes `ui/components/icon-paths.js`; a new icon is a new mapping line plus a regenerate (`tests/icon-paths.test.mjs` checks the file is in sync). Only the StudyHub mark stays hand-drawn. Light is thin: use 14px or more; `strokeWidth` above 1.6 emboldens a glyph (the heavy tick). No unicode or emoji stand-ins for icons (#1073).
- Typed glyphs (`tests/icon-stand-ins.test.mjs` scans every string of `ui/`, the English catalogues and every CSS `content`): a tick, cross, flag, refresh, plus, play, triangle or diagonal arrow (✓ ✕ ⚑ ↻ ＋ ▶ ▸ ↗ ○ ◆) is always a registry icon: `check`, `close`, `flag`, `refresh`, `plus`, `play`, `external`, `circle`, `diamond`. A "go on" arrow is `iconEnd="arrow-right"` on the Button (a Banner `action` takes `iconEnd` too), always on the right, whether the control is primary, secondary or a link; a way back is `icon="arrow-left"`, on the left; a button that already has its own leading icon (the sparkle of 生成) takes no arrow. A leading icon is for a control that acts where it stands (生成, 删除); a control that takes the learner somewhere ends in → with nothing in front (从这份资料出题, 练这一章, 创建题组, 打开书页), and the same words draw the same look wherever they appear: `tests/icon-round9.test.mjs` groups every control by its words and fails a second look, except the few words that name two different actions, listed with why (`LOOK_EXCEPTIONS` in `tests/helpers/go-on.mjs`, round 9). A row that ends in an arrow (a resume row, a notebook row) draws `<Icon name="arrow-right">` as its last cell. A label never carries the arrow, so a sentence that quotes a button (「点「发布并开始练」」) quotes the label only. Answer feedback is `<AnswerState>` (a `check` or `close` before the words); the circle before a fixed option (判断题: 对 / 错) shows its number, the key that picks it (`optionMark` in `ui/option-label.js`, drawn by `<OptionMark>`), never an icon. An icon inside running text carries `sh-icon--inline` and, on the side it sits, `sh-icon--lead` or `sh-icon--trail` (`ui/base.css`): the icon sits on the line like a letter and the gap is a token. What stays typed, and why: an arrow INSIDE a sentence is punctuation (「本组 40% → 55%」, 「A → B → C」, a step list, a lone ` → ` between two values) and is allowed because a string cannot hold a component; the guard fails only on an arrow at the start or the end of a string. × is the multiplication sign (between spaces, or before / after a number or placeholder, `×3`), never a close mark. A key name is typed on its keycap (← → ↑ ↓, Alt+↑), and the browser tab title is plain text: both are listed one by one in the test's `KEEP`, with why. A sentence that names a control names it in words (「点清除按钮」), not by its glyph: the thumbs are 「这题不错」 / 「这题有问题」, never 👍 / 👎, and no other emoji stands in for an icon either (the footnote back-link `↩︎` of the Markdown renderer is the one standard mark that stays, listed in the test's `KEEP`). The test also fails a Banner / EmptyState `action` or a conditional `icon` that holds `arrow-right` in front of the words (an `IconButton`, which has no words, is the exception).

## Colour roles

| Token | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `--bg-canvas` / `--bg-surface` / `--bg-raised` | Desk, flat panels, menus |
| `--paper`, `--paper-ink`, `--paper-dim`, `--paper-rule` | Card stock. Inside a card the ink tokens are re-scoped to paper values, once, in `ui/paper.css`: give an element `.sh-paper-card` (what `<Panel tone="paper">` renders; `.sh-paper` is the ink alone) and nothing else. |
| `--accent` (朱砂 cinnabar) | The single primary action, the card head rule / progress, the heatmap. Nothing else. |
| `--ok` jade | Correct, mastered |
| `--warn` ochre | Learning, contrasts to watch |
| `--bad` berry | Wrong, weak — deliberately far from cinnabar |

Coverage (questions asked of a section) adds no hue: covered is ink (`--text-dim`), planned and failed is ochre, never planned is an empty track; its square marks keep it apart from the round mastery marks (#1078, `ui/coverage/coverage.css`).

Rules: one filled primary button per screen; a primary that cannot act turns neutral, not muddy red; new CSS references tokens, never raw colours.

Accent presets (设置 › 界面 › 强调色): cinnabar stays the default and the identity; jade, ochre, graphite and plum are opt-in swaps of `--accent`, `--accent-soft` and `--accent-text` (`ui/accent.css`, one `data-accent` attribute), nothing else changes. `--bg-selected` is not one of them: a selected row is the same neutral warm surface on every accent (a selection tinted in the accent put a second red, green or plum on the page next to the primary button, #658); mark the selection with it plus a neutral check or edge, a "new" row with a `Badge`. `tests/ui-guardrails.test.mjs` counts every background, border or inset edge painted in `--accent` / `--accent-soft` / `--accent-glow` (rule `accentFill`, a per-file ratchet that may only fall; the allowed fills are listed in `scripts/qa/guardrail-baseline.mjs`). There is no free colour picker, so every preset is contrast-checked in `tests/accent-presets.test.mjs`, and none is blue-violet. The mastery hues (`--ok` jade, `--warn` ochre) keep their meaning; a preset that shares a hue with one is a choice of the learner, not a signal.

## Type

Three weights and two sizes carry the order of a page (#1079, `tests/type-weight-ratchet.test.mjs`). Before, 313 declarations were `--fw-strong` and a 550 medium that the Han fallback fonts drew as full bold, so a card title, a row title, a label and a count sentence all looked the same and nothing led.

- **strong** (`--fw-strong` 650): a page title, a card or section title, the one number a block is about, a badge, the current item of a row of steps or tabs. Strong is the exception on a page: if a block has more than one strong line, only the title stays.
- **medium** (`--fw-medium` 500): a row title, a field label, a tab, a button, a fold's summary. In Inter (digits and Latin) it is a real medium; in Han it is the regular face, so the difference to the text beside it comes from the ink colour (`--text` against `--text-dim`), not from a fake bold.
- **regular** (`--fw-regular` 400): a description, meta, a counts sentence, a hint, an empty state.
- **Why 500 and not 550**: Microsoft YaHei and the other Windows Han fallbacks have a regular and a bold face only. CSS font matching takes the heavier face for any weight above 500, so a 550 label was full bold in Chinese and a medium only in English. 500 maps to the lighter face. Never write a number between 400 and 700 for a Han label.
- **Size**: explanatory text (a description, a hint, a sentence of meta) is `--fs-sm` (13px) or more. `--fs-xs` (12px) is for badges, chips, key caps, counts and numbers, times, chart axes, code and the dense cells of a table, a tiny group label above a list; the ratchet's allow-list is `SMALL_OK` in `scripts/qa/type-weight-counts.mjs`.
- **Check**: `node scripts/qa/type-weight-counts.mjs` prints strong, medium, regular and small-type counts per file. The test lets each file's strong and small-type numbers only fall; it ends at under half of the 313 of 2026-11-17.

## Spacing

A 4px grid, `--space-1` … `--space-9` (4 8 12 16 20 24 32 40 56px), plus named half-steps for the values the grid skips. Write every `padding`, `margin`, `gap` and offset as a token; a raw px number is only for a hairline, a size that belongs to a specific control, or a one-off page measure.

| Need | Write |
| --- | --- |
| 2px hairline, badge padding | `--space-half` |
| a whole grid step | `--space-1` … `--space-9` (first choice; new work starts here) |
| 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 28px | `--space-1-5`, `--space-2-5`, `--space-3-5`, `--space-4-5`, `--space-5-5`, `--space-6-5`: halfway between the steps on either side, defined as `calc()` of them |
| 30px (32 less a hairline) | `--space-7-less` |
| the opposite of a step (a negative margin that cancels padding) | `calc(var(--space-N) * -1)` |

The half-steps exist so existing 6/10/14/18/22/28/30px layouts keep their exact size (`tests/space-half-steps.test.mjs` forbids them as raw px). Prefer a whole step in new rules; reach for a half-step only to match an existing neighbour or a control size that really needs it. Density (`data-density`) rescales `--space-1…9`, and the half-steps follow because they are derived from them. Left as raw px on purpose: `1px` hairlines and `-1px` border overlaps, sizes tied to a 34px / 44px control (indents beside an icon or checkbox), and the bespoke page-top and section measures of the review and results pages. `tests/ui-guardrails.test.mjs` ratchets the remaining raw spacing down per file.

- No boxed navigation, no rows of equal stat tiles, no ambient glow or glass.
- No side stripes: a card, row or note never gets a thick or tone-coloured left edge. Weak or strong is said by the meter's colour, the score or a badge; a quotation may keep a neutral rule beside it, with no box around it, and the rule has one width, 2px; a block of text that is not a quotation (a definition, a note, an overview) gets indent or a tint instead (`tests/side-stripes.test.mjs`, per-file ratchet).
- At most two container levels inside a page: the card, then one hairline or one quiet surface. Lists inside a card are rows under hairlines, not boxes in a box (#1080).
- Buttons (#1074–#1077, `tests/ui-buttons-round.test.mjs`):
  - A primary rests on its inner `--sheen` only: no accent glow or coloured shadow under it. Hover lifts it 1px on the neutral `--shadow-contact` and darkens the accent (`color-mix(... black 12%)`, never `--accent-soft`, which is light on dark and loses the ink's contrast on three presets); `tests/ui-buttons-round.test.mjs` computes the ink contrast of the hover on all five presets in both themes.

  - Every disabled button, secondary or primary, has the one neutral look (flat fill, no border, faint ink).
  - The practice bar at a narrow width is two rows from card edge to card edge: the tools, then 更多 with 上一题 / 下一题 sharing the rest of the row.

- No helper sentence where a number, label or layout already says it.
- Per-row actions (play, practise, select) appear on hover on pointer devices; information such as "继续 3/5" stays visible.

## Proactive elements on the question page

The question page keeps the learner's focus on answering the card; anything that appears without being clicked follows these five rules:

1. **Prefer ambient presence over triggers.** When something only needs to be found when wanted (such as a section link in the book, `#1197`, or a quiet time cue, `#1195`), place it calmly on the page instead of popping a tip or reminder.
2. **Bring actions closer instead of adding prose.** Put a direct action button on the surface rather than adding a paragraph explaining why the learner might want to act.
3. **Reserve prose for one situation.** Only speak up proactively with prose when the learner's own action just revealed a problem they are about to push into for nothing (such as `#1193`), and every item of that kind must satisfy all eight constraints:
   - Appears only immediately after a completed learner action, never mid-thought.
   - Never appears in mock exams (`exam`) or while the feature tour (`tourRunning`) is running.
   - At most one item shows at a time; the three first-use tips (`practice-tips.js`) always take priority.
   - Appears below the question as a quiet in-flow block, never a modal or popover covering the question or answer buttons.
   - Dismissible, with an explicit "stop showing this kind of tip" (`这类不再提示`) control.
   - Advisory only — never stops the round, moves cards, changes scheduling or intervals, or disables buttons.
   - Has an opt-out switch in `Settings › 引导` and an entry in `找功能`.
   - Never uses a filled primary button (`variant="primary"`).
4. **Trigger from concrete learner actions, never mental-state guesses.** Triggers come from what the learner just did (marking unknown terms, importing past exams, finishing a chapter), never from guessing frustration or fatigue from wrong-answer counts alone.
5. **Never spend model tokens automatically.** Proactive elements must never invoke the model on their own; any model call starts only after an explicit click (opt-in learner switches such as `#1204` are user preferences, not proactive offers).

# Late content

Data that arrives after a page is shown (coverage, recommendations, estimates, a second list) is the main source of "it jumps" and "it lags". Two rules, both checked by the journey:
- **Late content never moves what is already shown.** Reserve the space at the size it will have (a slot or skeleton of the same height, a badge slot at the end of a row's existing meta line), or replace in place, or place the new thing after the content that is already there. Never insert a block or a line above or between visible rows. A row's height does not depend on whether its extras have arrived.
- **Late content never changes a default the learner has already seen.** A default is decided once, from what was known when the area first showed. A later answer may update counts and add a quiet note (「找到 10 道同类题」) but does not switch the selected option, or the number and meaning of the button under the pointer. If the best default needs the late data, wait for it and render the area once, with a loading state.

The picker of 创建题组 follows this by reserving, not waiting: the page draws at once, as any page. Its index badge (one compact word, kept on a narrow row too because a touch screen has no hover to explain a lone mark; the pages in its hover) has its slot at the end of the facts line, in the last column of the row's grid, so the badges stand in one column. While the answer is on its way every row holds a box of fixed size there (`--picker-index-slot`, the widest word uncut), so a badge that comes fills it and moves nothing; once the answer is in, that column keeps its width on every row, badge or not (a row without one lends it to nothing), so the names on the facts line have the same room before and after the answer and none comes or goes when it lands. The facts are one line either way, so no row changes height: the course 课程范围 names is not said again on a row, another course comes first and takes the room, said whole or, short of room, shortened with 「…」 that ends on a word, never on its own 「·」 (its whole name on hover only while it is cut), and the names after it (more courses, the file a material comes from) follow it only where it is whole and they fit whole; which names show is one decision for the whole scope, from the words of every document in it and the line's width (`ui/picker-names.js`: on every row or on none; in 全部课程 every row keeps its course), so a run of rows reads the same and scrolling changes none, and a name not shown is still in the checkbox's name; and the format is always said, its pages and its size each whole or left out by that same decision (a document with pages says its size in pages first; in 全部课程 a row keeps its format beside its course where the course keeps room), and a note whole or out of sight, so a short row is one title line and one line of facts at 420 as at 1440. The first rows are drawn with `content-visibility: visible`, so the list's window has its real height in the first frame. The line that says how many materials are not fully indexed stands beside 添加资料 under the list (the 资料 page keeps its line above its own list), so a late one moves no row. A badge has no Tab stop of its own: the row's checkbox stands for it and opens its hover while it has the keyboard focus (#1084, `tests/generate-picker-sweep-browser.test.mjs` over a sweep of title lengths, `tests/generate-steady-browser.test.mjs` frame by frame with a slow answer, `tests/generate-picker-rows-browser.test.mjs`, `tests/generate-reach-browser.test.mjs`).

Late data also must not make the page heavy: memoize list rows so that a result for one row re-renders that row, not the list (the picker of 创建题组 and the 资料 page both do: a row holds the index badge's slot while the answer is on its way, and a row compares only its own data; `sourceRowPropsEqual`, `documentRowPropsEqual`). A status that already has a place (a 待发布 row's Badge, on the line of its facts) goes there rather than on a new line: a taller list moves everything below it further when it arrives.

The check: `scripts/qa/layout-stability.mjs` collects `layout-shift` (shifts within 500 ms of the learner's own input are excluded by Chromium's `hadRecentInput`) and `longtask` per step. `npm run qa:journey` prints and records per step `cls` and `longestTaskMs` in `summary.json`, and fails a step whose CLS is over 0.05 (`--cls-max`), naming the elements that moved; `--longtask-max <ms>` also fails a long task. `tests/layout-stability-guard.test.mjs` proves the tool catches a deliberate late insertion and stays quiet for a reserved slot.

Chromium's layout-shift score is small for a list that grows inside its own scroller (a row that is 20 px taller in a 120-row picker scored 0), so the observer also watches the rows of a `ScrollWindow` (`data-scroll-key`; any other list row can opt in with `data-stable-row`): a row whose height changes after it was first measured, other than within 500 ms of the learner's own input or a window resize, breaks the step ("list row height changed after it was shown"). `scripts/qa/layout-late.mjs` holds a late answer at the network edge on a seeded temporary library and measures the same elements before and after it arrives (picker with 132 materials, mistakes page, material names); `tests/layout-late-*.test.mjs` assert it.

# Confirmations

One question, one component. Pick by what the action does, not by where the button is.
- Destructive, irreversible or leaving-the-screen actions (delete a card, remove sample data, switch course and start over, submit an exam with blanks) use `ConfirmDialog`: a modal, cancel (quiet) first and focused, the confirm last (danger, or primary when nothing is lost). It stays open and busy while the action runs, shows a failure inside itself, cannot be dismissed while busy and fires once on a double click. The same action asks the same question from every entry point (the ⋯ menu and the editor both open the board's delete dialog).
- Reversible edits inside a form (reset a field group, merge two courses that can be split again, reload over unsaved input) use `InlineConfirm`: one boxed question in place, the same button order, focus on cancel when it appears and back on the trigger when it is cancelled. It is a labelled group, never `role="alertdialog"`, because the rest of the form stays usable.
- Never `window.confirm`, never "tap twice to confirm" by swapping a label, never a hand-built footer of two buttons.
- A destructive action does not stand in the row of the primary action with the same weight (#668): the row keeps the primary and at most one secondary button, and the destructive one is the item of a ⋯ `Menu` (danger colour, one step away) that then asks or offers 撤销 exactly as above. The draft page's bar (`ui/DraftBar.jsx`: 删除草稿) and a task's header (`ui/tasks/TaskMore.jsx`: 删除任务记录) are the two; a control that moves behind a menu keeps its 找功能 entry and a `data-tour` anchor on the menu button.
- An undo offer replaces a confirmation only when the action really is reversible: a toast with `undo: true` and an action leaves after its timeout but is held while the pointer or keyboard focus is on it.

Deleting has two tiers, decided by what is lost (owner, 3.7.0 D1):
- **Tier 1, the assets: 资料 (sources) and 题目 / 题组 (questions, decks, the 斩题组).** Archive first (归档, restorable), and only an archived item can be deleted for good, through `PermanentDeleteDialog` (a `ConfirmDialog` that names what goes and what stays). The archive itself says so in a notice with 撤销 and 永久删除…, which opens that same dialog. Every entry to 归档 (the Manage page, a deck row's ⋯ menu, a source row, 归档所选 for the ticked sources) ends in that one notice (`ui/archive-entries.js`); several sources at once have no single dialog, so their notice has 撤销 only and each is deleted for good from 已归档. The chat is told the same boundary in words: ask first, in one sentence, before any permanent delete or archive (`ASK_BEFORE_REMOVING`, `tests/chat-ask-before-removing.test.mjs`). A draft (待发布) is in between: its questions are unpublished but cost tokens, so its deletion asks once, in `DeleteDraftDialog` (the draft page and the 待发布 row open the same one; cancel is 「保留」).
- **Tier 2, everything else.** Moved so far (3.7.0 D1): task records and a day of 为你定制, 学习笔记, 学习流 templates and records, a kept annotation thread, PDF conversion records and 清空历史, archived live classes, interactive diagrams. ONE click, no dialog: the item leaves at once and the notice says 「已删除 …（8 秒内、下一次删除前可撤销）· 撤销」 (the app has one notice: the next deletion sends the one before it, so the words say so) (`ui/use-undoable.js` over `ui/undoable.js`). An operation with no inverse is sent only when that notice leaves (`commitLater`: its time ran out, it was closed or replaced, the page or the library changed, the page was hidden or closed); one with an inverse runs at once and 撤销 calls the inverse (`restoreWith`; an annotation thread is deleted at once by its ids and 撤销 restores exactly those). A failed deletion puts the item back with the reason; what the notice says keeps the facts a dialog used to say (working files that go, what stays). A deletion started from the keyboard keeps its notice until it is closed (no time limit, no time in the words), and 撤销 puts the focus back on the item. Sending on page hide/close is best effort: an ordinary call that the browser may cut off, in which case the item is simply still there. `InlineConfirm` is never used for an irreversible deletion.
- **Not moved yet** (they still ask in a dialog; candidates for the next steps): 删除骨架 (`ui/Skeleton.jsx`, irreversible: its dialog says 永久删除), 清除旧版本批注 (`annotation/StaleAnnotations.jsx`), 删除全部使用记录 (`UsageSettings.jsx`), 清空陪学画像 (`settings/CoachSection.jsx`), a board card (`board/DeleteCardDialog.jsx`, which has its own 恢复).
- A trigger that opens a confirmation may say 删除 (a 待发布 row's 「删除」, the draft page's 「删除草稿」); the dialog it opens then says 永久删除 in its title and its confirm, because that is the irreversible step.
- Words: **永久删除** = irreversible, no 撤销; **删除** = goes now, with 撤销 for a few seconds; **归档** = hidden, restorable; **移除** = taken out of a container (a card from a note, a step from a flow), nothing is deleted. `tests/delete-tiers.test.mjs` keeps a new irreversible deletion off `InlineConfirm` and off label swaps.
- Closing: `Dialog` `busy` means "work is running": the close button stays, is marked aria-disabled, Escape and the backdrop do nothing. Use `CloseButton` for any other close control and `Popover` / `Menu` / `Tooltip` for floating panels (they share `useDismiss` and `useAnchoredPosition`). A tooltip anchored to a chart's data point is the same surface placed from the point instead of from an anchor (`.sh-tooltip--point`, ui/charts/DashboardCharts.jsx): the chart's stylesheet writes no tooltip rule of its own.

# Fields and settings

A settings page is built from the same few parts; it never styles a label or a switch of its own.
- `SettingsSection` is the shell of a section (fieldset, legend title, lead, `tour` anchor). `Field` is one label + control + hint + error: it generates the id, wires `aria-describedby` / `aria-invalid` and adds the alert; `width` is `sm | md | full`, `inline` puts the label beside the control, `group` is for a control that names itself (SegmentedControl). One label weight (`--fw-medium`), one hint size (`Hint`, `--fs-sm`).
- Choosing one of many: `Select` for a short list with no search (type-ahead still works), `Combobox` when the list grows or has a tree: every course and deck picker (`ui/course-picker-entries.js` builds the course tree once from `rankCourses` / `groupCourseNames`). Both take `options` (`{ value, label, hint?, disabled?, level? }` or `{ group, options }`) and sit inside `Field` like any control. An action is never an option: "课程设置…" and "新建题组「…」" are Combobox `actions`, buttons under the list. Selection is `--bg-selected` + a check, never the accent. The popup takes `--shadow-popover` and concentric option corners. Behaviour comes from Base UI (`@base-ui/react`), imported only in `select-impl.jsx` / `combobox-impl.jsx` behind lazy facades, so Base UI loads on first use and the closed trigger draws at once; the shared filtering and highlighting is pure (`option-list.js`). There is no native `<select>` in `ui/` (a `<datalist>` is fine). In the DSH host `react` and `react-dom` both come from the host (React 18, `scripts/host-react.mjs`); never bundle a second react-dom.
- `TextInput`, `TextArea`, `NumberInput` render `.sh-input`, and the Select / Combobox trigger wears the same look; the bare `input` / `select` rules in base.css are legacy (they only reach elements without an `sh-` class) and are not relied on. `Checkbox` is a labelled row and `CheckboxInput` the same box alone, for a row that lays out its own label (a picker, tree or pill row; `indeterminate` for the mixed state): every visible checkbox in `ui/` is one of the two, drawn from the tokens (`tests/shared-checkbox.test.mjs`). `Switch` is the same row with `role="switch"` and a track (for a setting that acts at once), `RadioCard` + `RadioCardGroup` for a few choices that deserve a sentence each. `ProviderCard` + `StepList` are the card of a service that needs a key. A settings page never writes a checkbox of its own: `tests/ui-guardrails.test.mjs` fails on a `type="checkbox"` / `type="radio"` in a settings file, on a `role="switch"` outside `ui/components` (the course switch in `ui/CourseActive.jsx` is the one left) and on a retired switch-row class (`.inline-check`, `.experimental-switch`, `.jev-switch`, `.usage-switch`, `.mineru-privacy__check`) coming back to a stylesheet.
- A settings category is declared once in `ui/settings-groups.js`: title, group, anchors, `needs` (the host component it requires), `partNeeds` (one gated part inside a category that stays visible) and its pane, loaded lazily. `Settings` renders `<category.Component {...services} />` and decides nothing else per category.

## Stylesheets

`ui/style.css` is gone. The global sheets are listed in `ui/styles.js` in cascade order: `tokens.css` (theme, fonts, the semantic tokens), `paper.css`, `base.css` (element defaults), `legacy.css` (old buttons and page furniture; do not add), `shell.css` (sidebar, top bar), the feature sheets beside their components (`review/question.css`, `review/session.css`, `study-map/desk.css`, `study-map/catalog.css`, `draft.css`, `sources-page.css`, `audio-import.css`, `binding.css`, `markdown.css`, `followup.css`) and `motion.css`. A feature's rules go in its own sheet; `tests/css-structure.test.mjs` fails when a feature selector lands in tokens, base or shell.

Every sheet is wrapped once: `@layer study.<layer> { :is(.study-app, .study-seat) { ... } }`. Layers, lowest to highest: `reset` (element defaults, motion), `tokens`, `components` (ui/components, legacy.css), `features`, `overrides` (host chrome). A feature beats a component because of its layer, not because of a longer selector; !important inverts the order, so keep it to the motion switches. Root rules (the theme attributes, the app box) keep their own selector; host-world sheets (host/studyhub.css, panel-bridge.css, the unscoped part of document-preview.css) are the only exceptions and are listed in `scripts/qa/css-layers.mjs`. `node scripts/qa/css-layers.mjs wrap <file>` wraps a new sheet; `node scripts/qa/css-layers.mjs check` lists problems. One hook injects a sheet (`useComponentCss`, also exported as `useInjectCss`), and once per document by marker.

Checks that keep it so: `node scripts/qa/dead-css.mjs` (a class nothing renders), `tests/css-ownership.test.mjs` (a class is written by one sheet; the allow-list only shrinks), `scripts/qa/css-fingerprint.mjs` (computed styles of every page before and after a stylesheet refactor).

# Feature finder (找功能)

One list says what a learner can look up and where it leads: `ui/finder/entries.js`, plain data like `ui/pages.js` (Chinese source strings, English in `ui/locales/en.finder.json`). Where a registry already says it, the entry is derived and nothing is copied: one entry per page of `PAGES` that has a sidebar group or a purpose, one per category of `SETTINGS_CATEGORIES` (its `purpose` is the sentence) and one per `GLOSSARY` term. The features inside a page are hand-written (`FEATURES`). Kinds: `page`, `setting`, `feature`, and `term`, which shows a meaning and has nowhere to go. An entry has a title, `aliases` (old names, synonyms, English words), a path as crumbs, one sentence that is true for the code, `anchors` (`data-tour` ids, the control first, then what stands in for it until the page shows it), `needs` (`model`, `capability`, `flag`, `install`: said as tags; a missing capability or host switch greys the entry with the reason, it is never hidden) and `prepare` (what the learner does first when the control is not on screen, because the finder cannot open a document, start a round or open a menu for them).

**Every later fold, move or rename keeps the control findable**: a control that leaves its page, goes behind a menu or changes its name keeps (or gets) an entry here, with the old name as an alias and its new place as the anchor. `tests/feature-finder-entries.test.mjs` fails on a page, a settings category or a glossary term without an entry, on an anchor that is no longer in the UI source (`tests/helpers/tour-anchors.mjs`, the scan the tour test uses) and on a string without English. A new `data-tour` attribute is the one change to an existing page the finder needs; a settings anchor is also listed in the `anchors` of its category in `ui/settings-groups.js`, because that is what makes a link select the category.

Surface: one `Dialog` with a `TextInput` and a listbox (↑ ↓ move, Enter is 带我去, Esc closes and gives the focus back). With nothing typed the list is the index, each area a `<details>` with the shared chevron; a query is one list ranked by how well each entry matches, on the Chinese and on the English rendering (`ui/finder/search.js`). The selected row is `--bg-selected`, never the accent; 带我去 is a secondary button, shown on the selected row and on hover. The list has a fixed height, so it does not grow and shrink with every key. Keys are read on the app's own root element, never on `window` or `document` (Ctrl K / ⌘K anywhere in the app, `/` outside a text field; nothing opens over another dialog or the tour).

The trip (`ui/finder/use-finder.js`): go to the page (a settings anchor is a settings link), wait for the anchor as the tour does (`ui/tour/anchor.js`), open the `<details>` around it, scroll it to the middle and give it the focus. No ring is drawn around it (removed 2026-11-05: superfluous, and with folds and drawers moving things it was often in the wrong place). When the control could not simply be lit, a toast says where it is and what is left to do.
