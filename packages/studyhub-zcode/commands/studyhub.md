---
description: 打开 StudyHub 学习面板（复习、题组、课程进度） / Open the StudyHub study panel
---

调用 `studyhub_open` 工具打开 StudyHub 学习面板。然后用学习者的语言告诉他：用的是哪个学习库（`root`）、今天有多少题到期待复习（`dueToday`），并提醒面板已在右侧栏打开；如果没看到，在右侧栏右上角的「添加标签页」菜单里找 StudyHub 学习中枢；要把侧栏切到主区域全视图，按 `Ctrl+Shift+F`（macOS `Cmd+Shift+F`），或点侧栏右上角的展开按钮。

Call the `studyhub_open` tool to open the StudyHub panel. Then tell the learner, in their language, which library is in use (`root`) and how many cards are due for review today (`dueToday`), and that the panel is open in the right side pane; if they do not see it, it is in the side pane's "add tab" menu as StudyHub; `Ctrl+Shift+F` (`Cmd+Shift+F` on macOS) or the side pane's expand button shows it in the main area.
