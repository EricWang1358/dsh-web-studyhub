- **Course outline and review book builds no longer hit a hidden 8-minute single-call cap, failed calls state their reason in the job log, and time limits can be adjusted or set to no limit (#1176).** Direct gateway calls previously inherited the card assistant's 8-minute timeout, cutting slow models during Inventory or book writing at 8 minutes even while they were still streaming output, with no reason in the job log and no setting to change it. Single model calls in outline and review book builds now have no wall-clock limit by default (as long as output keeps streaming; 3 minutes with no output at all still cuts automatically), and failed calls state their cause in the job log (reaching the single-call limit, 3-minute silent cut, or provider status and error code). Outline and review book tasks now show the Limit strip and Adjust time budget link on the Jobs page just like question tasks, and in Settings › Question defaults both Time budget per round and the new Single-call time limit for outline & book support custom minutes or `0` (Set no limit). Not verified: end-to-end desktop runs against a live slow reasoning provider streaming past 8 minutes (tested with fake streaming models and timers).
