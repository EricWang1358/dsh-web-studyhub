- **Added an opt-in option to separate wrong and skipped questions in the written exam report, with source links (off by default).** When you turn on "Split wrong & skipped in written exam report with source links" under Settings > Practice, a written exam report with unanswered items shows your accuracy across answered questions on its own line, adds separate "Practise wrong" and "Practise unanswered" buttons next to the combined practice button, and puts "Open source" and "Practise this question" on each missed or skipped question (using no model tokens). Leaving the setting off keeps the report unchanged. Not verified: live browser layout across mobile and desktop widths.
