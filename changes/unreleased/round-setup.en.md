- **You can set up one round before you start it: its size, its range and one chapter.** Beside “Start today's study” on the home page and the next-round button on the result page there is now a small gear, “This round”: pick 10 / 20 / 30 questions or type your own (5–50, the same range as the round size in Settings › Practice), choose Mixed / Due / New / Mistakes, optionally practise one chapter only, or jump to the reader to choose by page. A change affects this round only; “Make default” saves the size to Settings › Practice, which then shows the same number. Each course remembers the last choice and the gear opens on it next time. Without touching the gear the start button and the number of clicks are exactly what they were. Also, the most new questions a round of new questions may ask for (for the assistant and scripts) comes down from 100 to 50, the ceiling of the round size. Not verified: in the DSH host (checked in the browser preview with sample data only), with a screen reader, and how the chapter list feels in a course with dozens of chapters.
