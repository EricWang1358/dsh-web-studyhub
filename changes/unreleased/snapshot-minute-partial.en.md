- **An idle library no longer downloads itself again every minute.** Left open on any page with nothing written, StudyHub used to fetch the whole library once a minute (about 277 KB with 300 materials) only because due counts move with the clock. Now that minute's check brings back only the parts that move with the clock (today's plan, due counts per deck, progress, focus, material mastery, archived tasks) and of those only what changed, usually about 1 KB; materials, questions and everything else stay as they are on screen. Anything you or a task writes still brings the whole library, and so does the first check of a new day. Not verified: the saving inside a real DSH window over a long session (it was measured with a 300-material test library in a browser harness).
