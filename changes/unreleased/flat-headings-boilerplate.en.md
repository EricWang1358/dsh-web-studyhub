- **A converted PDF is chaptered by its headings again, and lines repeated on most of its pages are left out.** MinerU writes every title at one level, so a book came out with one "chapter" per page; StudyHub now reads the levels from the titles' numbering (Part I / Chapter 2 / 1.1 / 1.1.1, and the Chinese schemes) and keeps the converter's levels when the titles carry no numbering. A `# …` comment inside code that is not fenced (`x = 1` / `# a note` / `print(x)`) is no longer read as a heading, so a run no longer plans a section for it and spends rounds finding nothing to ask. Lines on at least 3 pages and 40% of a document (running headers, footers, page numbers) are dropped from the converted text; the original file is untouched, and the conversion's row says how many went, with one example and a Restore link. Headings, formulas, code, tables, pictures and labels such as "Solution:" are never dropped. Books built before this keep their chapters until you convert the PDF again; the book then shows it is out of date, and its next Update rebuilds the outline. Not verified: tested with fake MinerU output and a stand-in host, not with a real MinerU run or a real course PDF.
