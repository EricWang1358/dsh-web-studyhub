- **What you highlight in the reader comes back when you are weak there.** Passages you highlighted or bolded come back as one quiet "Re-read (N)" line in Today, under the day's button (the button never moves when it comes). They appear after 3 days without a look, when their chapter or page has due or weak questions, riskiest first, at most 3 a day (#1018). A click opens the reader at the passage. There, "Re-read done" records it and moves to the next one, and "Practise this section" starts that section's questions. Settings › Practice can turn the line off. On a made-up library of 10 books of 500 pages the first answer takes about 50 ms and later ones about 5 ms. Not verified: a real large library.
