- **Desktop: agy can be started ahead of time, so replies arrive sooner.** In Settings › Library & model, the local CLI card has a new choice, "Start agy ahead of time (pre-warm)": Off, After use (the default: after a call, the next agy waits up to 10 minutes) or Always. The card says what each costs (about 200 MB of memory while one waits) and who it suits. In a few runs on one computer a one-word reply went from about 11 to 20 seconds to about 5 to 10; the model's own thinking time is unchanged. Each waiting process answers once, so questions never share a conversation, and "Test connection" always starts a new one. Desktop only: the terminal edition still starts agy for every call. Not verified: the packaged desktop window, other computers and agy versions, an agy left waiting longer than 30 minutes, and macOS or Linux with the real agy.
