- **Every question on the draft page is one row of the same shape.** The number, the question (cut at two lines, whole once you open it) and the kind sit in one line; a flag such as “Not reviewed automatically” goes after the kind. The first question no longer has its number on a line of its own, and a closed row is about 46 to 66 pixels high instead of 120 to 145, so a draft of many questions no longer takes a long scroll. On a narrow screen the kind goes under the question. Not verified: only in a Chromium preview (1440 and 420 pixels wide) with short and long questions, not inside DSH.
