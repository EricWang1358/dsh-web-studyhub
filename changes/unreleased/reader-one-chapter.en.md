- **The reader now shows one chapter at a time, so a 500-page book opens in a moment instead of freezing for seconds.** The chapters are the same ones the Sources row lists under "Show N chapters" (a document without chapters reads in parts of 20 pages). The Contents lists the chapters with their pages folded inside, and Previous chapter / Next chapter sit at the bottom. Every way to a page still lands on it: Contents rows and its filter, "Back to this question" from a citation, practice links back to the source, "Show N chapters", the reading position you left, and Find, which also lists the other chapters that contain your words with the count you will find there; Enter and Shift+Enter carry on into the next chapter, or back to the last match of the one before. With a kept AI outline, the mastery marks of every chapter stay in the Contents, and Back to source from a practice run reopens the chapter you left. Formulas are typeset only when they come near the screen, and the reading position is followed without measuring every Contents row on each scroll. On a real 513-page, 25-chapter book (development build, 1280 px): the longest freeze went from 9.1 s to 0.36 s, total blocked time from 21.5 s to 0.66 s, and the page from 60,411 to 1,663 elements. Not verified: coming back from the Original PDF tab to that page's chapter (no PDF file in the test), chapters a learner set by hand, "Translate this chapter" on a chaptered book, the production build's numbers, and that the new browser test fails on the old reader.
