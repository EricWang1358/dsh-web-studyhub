- **You can answer flashcards, open questions and cloze questions by voice, and an AI grade now arrives once, with a correction you read before the retry.** Press "Answer by voice" under the question, or V, and press again when you finish: the recording becomes text in the answer box and is deleted, and you submit it like typing. For a cloze, say "first blank …, second blank …" and the blanks are filled; a cloze is then graded exactly as if you had typed it. With Auto-advance on, a spoken open question is sent for AI grading and you move on; the question is not offered again while it is graded. A miss whose grade lands after you left waits under "Corrections to read" in the practice header and on the result page; "Got it · add a retry" puts it at the end of the round. An AI grade can be changed once it is in, and the review interval follows; the AI's own grade is kept. Flashcards stay self-graded unless a course turns on "Flashcards can be AI-graded too" in its settings. The mock interview takes speech the same way, reads the course's job description for its follow-ups, and shows the same correction in its report. Shift+V says your question into "Help me understand". Voice needs a transcription service in Settings › Audio transcription, or the live class's local engine; without one, V opens that setup. Not verified: a real microphone, a real cloud transcription service or the real local engine (checked with a simulated microphone, a stand-in transcription and the fake model only), and the quality of real AI grades and corrections.
