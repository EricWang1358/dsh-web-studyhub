- **A big book can be built a few chapters at a time, before any outline, and the review book makes only the parts your custom plan ticks.** Pick chapters of the material (what **Customize in depth** saves) and the first build reads only their pages, organises only them and writes their points; the outline lists every other chapter as **Not built · pages a–b**. A chapter added later joins the same outline without rewriting or paying again for the ones already written (on a 500-page test book, two chapters took 10 inventory calls instead of 46). Without term cards, confusable pairs or answer templates in the plan their model calls are not made, and the formula page, the index and the cheat sheet are not offered on the book page until you tick them again in **Your custom plan**; a course without a plan builds exactly as before, and the estimate counts the inventory calls the pick would really make. Not verified: a real 500-page book, a real model, the main chat applying the plan, or a material whose chapters change between two builds.
