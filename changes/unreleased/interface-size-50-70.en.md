- **The interface size now goes down to 50% and 70%.** Pick them under Settings → Interface → Interface size; the choice is kept after a reload. Below 100% more fits on the screen and the text gets smaller: at 50% most text is 6–7.5 px and most buttons are under 24 px, which is hard to read on a screen at 100% system scaling; at 70% text is about 8.4–12 px. Change it back in the same place. Fit window still only scales up. At 70% or 50% a 420 px panel has room for the docked sidebar, so it shows that instead of the menu button (the sidebar's collapse button still turns it into the drawer). Also fixed, wrong at every size other than 100%: the divider of the source column jumped when dragged; dragging the course graph, a node of the concept canvas or a sidebar row did not keep up with the pointer; the term-explanation hover card in a document opened away from its passage; the statistics chart was drawn half as wide as its box, or past it; the outline filter bar stuck in the wrong place; areas sized by the window height (such as an embedded web original in a source, or the course graph) were half as tall as meant or ran past the window; and a narrow panel flashed the menu button before showing the sidebar. Going back to an older version: it does not know 50% or 70% and shows its default size (100% in the plugin, Fit window on desktop); as long as you change no appearance setting there, your choice is still there after upgrading again.
