- **The review book has a cheat sheet you can print or save (#710).** **More › Cheat sheet** shows one dense two-column page for an open-book exam: the answer templates and the question-signal table, the formulas (drawn properly) and every numbered term card by chapter, and the pairs of easily confused concepts side by side once the book has them. Each entry says which point of the book it comes from and the page of the material; choose a point's number to go back to it in the book, and a card number to jump to that card. **Print / save as PDF** prints it as clean two-column A4 pages without the app around it, and **Download .html** (also in **More › Export… › Cheat sheet (.html)**) saves one file that opens offline on a computer or a phone. It is made from the book as it is, with no model call, and it only appears once the book has term cards, answer templates or formulas. Not verified: seen with fake books in Chromium (an 840-card book lays out in about half a second and prints to about 40 pages); not seen with a real model's book, in other browsers' print dialogs, on a real phone, or inside DSH. The pairs of confusable concepts are drawn when the book carries them; the step that writes them is a separate change.
