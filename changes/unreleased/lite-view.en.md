- **A new “Lite view” switch keeps only what daily practice needs in the sidebar and on the library home.** It is at the top of Settings › Interface, off by default and kept only in this browser. While it is on, the sidebar's main list shows the library, materials, question creation and mistakes (the ones you keep there); every other page is under “More”, the “Personalised” row is not shown and rows cannot be reordered (your saved order is kept). The next-step card stays only to remind you of an exam within a week or a new material with no questions yet. The home leaves out “What to do first today”, the final-week and this-week cards, course setup, the extra lines beside the course title (outline button, progress, study method, focus topics, mastery, recommended next step), the paper-fold and draw-a-card strip, the outline and graph buttons of the catalogue and the topic-group reminder. The exam countdown stays; the cross-workspace notebook list stays too, folded to its heading, because publishing and searching it have no other way in. The Today card, drafts waiting to be published, generation progress and failures, the course switcher and the deck list stay, and when the next-step card is not on the page, “Not sure where to start?” waits under “Other ways to start”. One click at the foot of the home, “Switch to full view”, brings everything back; the guided tour always shows the full view; resetting the appearance leaves the switch as it is; nothing is deleted and the review schedule does not change. Find a feature knows the switch, and when its directions go through a button lite view tucked away, it says so and offers the full view. The practice page, the result page and the top bar are unchanged. Not verified: a real DSH window, the desktop app, a touch device, a screen reader, and a library that has the final-week or this-week cards in a real browser.
