- **Typing in the terminal's translation glossary is no longer wiped by a reload.** If you pressed `G` before the reader's page had arrived and then `a` to add a term, the glossary replaced its whole body with a spinner and loaded again when the page came in, and everything typed meanwhile was lost. Loading the same document again now happens in the background: the field and what you typed stay put, a failed refresh keeps what was shown and says why, and a term saved during a refresh is not overwritten by an older answer. Until the first load has finished, the keys that add, delete or switch language are ignored and not offered in the footer (pressing `a` used to open a field you could not see). Not verified: checked only with the virtual terminal and sample data of the automated tests, not in a real terminal window, against a slow real library or over SSH.
