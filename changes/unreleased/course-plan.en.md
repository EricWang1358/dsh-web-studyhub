- **The review book page can now show a study plan (备考计划) for the whole period up to the exam, and the final week takes over at the end.** The plan is drafted with the AI in the main chat and saved on the course only when you agree: with no plan the page shows one quiet line with **Send to the main chat** (and **Set the exam date** when the course has none), and there is no form. Once saved, a section under the book bar shows the countdown, how many points are learnt and placed, the points that did not fit, the phases with their goals, today's day and the next seven, and **Refresh**. It folds to one row (kept per course). It is made by the program from the review book and your answers, with no model call, and made again every day from what you really did. The two plans read as one flow: the study plan covers the whole period, and from the day the final week begins the daily part is the final week's (one line points at **Today's points**), so no day is shown twice. The main chat can read it (`course.plan`) and save it (`course.save` with `plan`, on request). Idea and port from the community user BAXUIE's StudyHub for ZCode, thank you! Not verified: with a real DSH chat (the hand-off message was only checked as text and the clipboard path in the preview, and the chat never saved a plan); a plan longer than a year; a library with a book of many points (checked with a book of two).
