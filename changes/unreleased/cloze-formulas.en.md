- **Fill-in-the-blank questions draw their formulas, and new ones no longer hide the blank inside a formula.** A fill-in-the-blank card used to show every formula as raw `$…$` text, and the correct answer under a wrong one as well; both are now drawn like the formulas on every other card, even on a card whose blank sits inside a formula. New fill-in-the-blank questions keep the blank in the words of the sentence and ask for a word, name or number you can type, not a formula; one written that way is sent back for one correction before it reaches the draft. Not verified: how often a real model still needs that correction; fill-in-the-blank cards made before this change that ask for a formula still expect that formula as typed text, so make them again or edit them.
