- **Formulas now draw completely, with the standard LaTeX look, on every machine and offline: \mathbb{E}, \mathcal{F}, bold vectors, fractions, matrices, stretchy brackets and chemistry.** Before, formulas relied on the browser's native MathML and whatever math font the machine had; Chromium ignores the letter styles, so \mathbb, \mathcal, \mathbf and \mathfrak came out as plain letters. Formulas are now laid out by the bundled KaTeX with its own fonts (packed into the app, no download), in questions, options, explanations, the reader, notes, the review book, the cheat sheet and the saved .html pages, which carry the fonts so they read offline. The fonts load only with the first formula and never hold up the page, and an inline formula no longer makes its line taller. Not verified: inside the DSH host window itself (checked in Chromium with the app's styles), and printing the saved pages on paper.
