(4) After you answer, the feedback sits at the foot of the card in plain words: “Correct · see it again in about 3 d”; the interval and the memory-stability numbers are behind “Why 3 d”, closed until you open it, each with a plain explanation. (5) The column of short lines on the left of the practice page now says what each one is when you hover it or reach it with the keyboard (“Question 2 · topic · Answered right · Mastery: Learning”); clicking one still jumps to that question, a retry stop is drawn dotted, and on a narrow window (phone width) the column is gone so the card keeps its width.
  - **Following a letter from the Inbox to a question to read its explanation no longer lets Auto-advance take you away.** You open a notice, land on a question you answered right, to read the “Help me understand” or follow-up answer; even with Auto-advance on, it stays on that question until you have read it, and it works as before once you use “Back to question N”. The switch itself is not turned off.
