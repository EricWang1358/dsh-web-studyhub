- **There are now sentences you can say to start AI-guided learning.** The tour's guided-study step gives one you can type as it is and points to Settings › Guidance › AI-guided learning. That section lists ready-made sentences ("Teach me conditional probability and test me with a question after each point", "Teach and test me on chapter 3 of my notes", "I got this question wrong: explain why first, then give me a similar one", "Continue my last guided study"), each with "Send to the main chat", and says which three things a guided session leaves off until you agree: new questions on the spot, filing Q&A, and editing notes. The guided workbench shows the same sentences when it has no question. The workbench's words (buttons, progress, sources, status) now follow the interface language, and it no longer shows raw states such as "active". Not verified: how it looks in a DSH window and how a screen reader reads it (the rendered text and what is sent to the main chat were tested).
