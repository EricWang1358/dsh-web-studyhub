- **The review book can be built a few chapters at a time (#708).** On the course outline page, **Build only some chapters** sits folded beside **Write the review book**, which still writes the whole book in one click. It lists the outline's chapters, each marked **Not built**, **Built a/b** or **Built**. Tick some and, before anything starts, the page says how many knowledge points that writes, about how many model calls and about how many tokens of material the calls read, with the tip about cheaper ways; no prices. Only the picked chapters' points are written and only their materials are read. Every other point is kept as it is, so a chapter built later never rewrites or re-pays one already written. A pick of 12 points or more writes its two-point sample from those chapters, and **Build the other N** stays inside them. A book of some chapters is no longer shown as out of date: its line says how many chapters are not built, their rows say **Not built**, and the whole-book button reads **Build all the other chapters**. Picking needs an outline first (**Generate outline** only reads the materials and arranges the chapters). Not verified: checked with a fake model and in the preview at 1440 and 420 px in light mode, in Chinese; not with a real model, not in dark mode or English on screen, not inside DSH. The token figure counts only the material read, so a real run uses more.
