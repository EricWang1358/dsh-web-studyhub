- **A converted book whose sections are numbered like 1—1 gets its real chapters.** Some textbooks (often translations) title a chapter with no "Chapter N" and number its sections 1—1, 1-2, 2–1 …; StudyHub cut such a book into dozens of one-page "chapters", and an appendix heading could swallow hundreds of pages. Now each chapter starts at the title before its first section (past "本章要点" and the like), the pages before chapter 1 are front matter, a contents page and captions such as 表1-1 are not sections, an answer key at the end that repeats the section numbers stays with the last chapter, chapter titles numbered like "1. 绪论" stay chapters, and each chapter keeps its own end-of-chapter questions and 附录. Headings such as "1-2 题" or "9-15 Lecture" in notes and schedules are read as before. A book you already imported shows the new chapters in its chapter list, its coverage, the course setup and 深度定制 without converting it again; a book already built from it shows it is out of date. A 深度定制 plan saved before keeps its chapters where they read the same; a chapter that changed is not swapped for another one: you are asked to pick it again (changing a switch on the 你的定制 card does not count as picking it). Not verified: tested with made-up books and the heading lines of the real 513-page book (read only, 24 chapters), not with a real MinerU run or in the app on a real library.
