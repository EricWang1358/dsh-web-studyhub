- **A converted book whose sections are numbered like 1—1 gets its real chapters.** Some textbooks (often translations) title a chapter with no "Chapter N" and number its sections 1—1, 1-2, 2–1 …; StudyHub cut such a book into dozens of one-page "chapters", and an appendix heading could swallow hundreds of pages. Now each chapter starts at the title before its first section (past "本章要点" and the like), the pages before chapter 1 are front matter, captions such as 表1-1 are not sections, and each chapter keeps its own end-of-chapter questions and 附录. A book you already imported shows the new chapters in its chapter list, the course setup and 深度定制 without converting it again; a book already built from it shows it is out of date.
