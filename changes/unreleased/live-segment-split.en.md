- **Live class (Gemini) now cuts one long block of speech into sentences, and Chinese is no longer "translated" into Chinese.** When Gemini delivered a long stretch of text in one piece, it became a single sentence thousands of characters long. It is now cut at every full stop, question mark and exclamation mark inside it (fragments that are too short join the next sentence), and where there is no punctuation at about 300 characters after the nearest comma or space, so each sentence can be ticked, polished and used for questions on its own. Simplified Chinese sentences are no longer queued for translation and no longer show "Translating…"; Traditional Chinese and English are still translated. Not verified: only tried with simulated recognition results, not with a real Gemini class or real long pieces.
