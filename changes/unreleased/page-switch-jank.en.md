- **Switching pages is smooth again.** The page you leave now fades by transparency and movement only, so a switch no longer restyles every element of that page in the click. A few appearance rules (the Animation and high-contrast settings, the rows of 待发布) no longer make the browser restyle the whole app each time something new appears on screen; inside DSH the animation and contrast settings still reach the note and the question picker shown beside StudyHub. Measured in the preview with a large library, switching between seven pages three times at 1440 and 1050 px: the median wait from a press to the next frame fell from about 26 and 33 ms to about 10 and 11 ms, the longest from 71 and 103 ms to 25-34 ms, and tasks over 50 ms from about 25 to 7 per run (about 1,600 ms to 450 ms). Not verified: measured in headless Chromium in the standalone preview (React 19), not inside DSH (React 18), not with the sidebar as a drawer, not on other widths; the seat's own animation and contrast attributes were checked in the preview, not in DSH.
