- **When the browser refuses to share a tab, the live class now says what happened on your system and gives the next step as buttons.** The message no longer sends Windows users to a screen-recording permission that does not exist there. It names the real causes (a work or school policy, an app's built-in window, or the macOS permission), offers **Switch to microphone and start** and, where trying again can work, **Try sharing again**, and a **What the browser reported** line lists what the browser said. On Windows it also says how to record the computer's sound without sharing ("Stereo Mix" or a virtual cable as the default recording device), which works from an app window and with headphones. Not verified: in a real app window, on a managed machine or on macOS (these cases are tested with simulated refusals).
