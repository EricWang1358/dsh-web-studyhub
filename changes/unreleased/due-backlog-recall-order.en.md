- **Daily rounds over large due backlogs prioritise still-remembered cards (#881)**
  When building an unscoped daily round with more overdue cards than fit in the round limit, StudyHub keeps highlighted (`focus`) questions first, surfaces cards whose `last_review` timestamp is missing (`recall === 0`) next so they cannot starve indefinitely, and then orders remaining due cards by descending predicted FSRS-6 recall (earlier due date breaking ties) while evaluating `retrievabilityAt` once per due card. When `dueTotal >= roundSize * 3`, the home plan also displays an estimated catch-up pace (`{0} due — about {2} days at {1} a day; starting with the ones you still remember`).
  Not verified: multi-week learner retention outcomes under large backlogs (checked via unit ordering, 3,000-card performance budget, and `zh`/`en` home-plan rendering tests).
