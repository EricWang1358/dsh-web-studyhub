- **“Find a feature” in the sidebar looks a feature up by name and takes you there.** Above “Feature tour” there is a new row (the shortcut is Ctrl K, ⌘K on a Mac, or / when no text field has the focus; it is also in the keyboard shortcut sheet). Type a word in Chinese, English or a common name (“translate”, “spending limit”, “autopilot”) and pick a result: it shows where the feature is, as a path, in one sentence, with what it needs (an AI model, DSH turning something on, an install). With nothing typed the sheet is the whole index by area, so it also answers “where is everything”. “Take me there” opens the page, opens the fold the control is in, scrolls to it, puts the focus on it and draws a ring around it for a moment; if the control only exists after something else (an open material, a round of practice, a draft), it takes you to the right page and a message says what is left to do. A feature this DSH cannot reach is shown greyed with the reason. The tour’s sidebar step now points at it. Not verified: the sidebar row and the sheet were checked in a Chromium preview only (1440 and 420 pixels wide, Chinese and English), not inside DSH itself, and a feature that sits behind an open material or a running round of practice was checked for its message, not walked through to the control.
