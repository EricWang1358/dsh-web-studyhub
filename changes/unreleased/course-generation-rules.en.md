- **Each course can keep up to 12 generation rules of its own, and every run for the course follows them.** A rule is one sentence of at most 200 characters, such as "every stem carries its own scenario and conditions". Knowledge extraction, answer preparation, writing and the independent review all receive the rules, and the requirements preview before a run lists them on a row of their own. While practising, press "Make it a course rule" under the Improve question box or beside the 👎 tags; the confirmation shows the course and the exact rules before anything is saved. See, change or delete the rules in the course settings under More settings › Generation rules, or look them up in Find a feature. After a change, a draft reviewed under the old rules is reviewed again in reviewed publication, and Publish and practise publishes its questions as not automatically reviewed. The review stage of a normal run now also receives the run's other generation settings, which it was missing before. Not verified: that the rules make the questions better; in a real DSH window with a real model; case papers do not take the rules, and a parent course does not pass its rules to a chapter.
