- **A quick-add on Today is no longer refused right after the page opens.** Opening Board → Today with an active course plan makes the daily plan prepare its own proposal, and that writes the board. A card you typed in that moment was refused as if someone else had changed the board: the text stayed in the box, no card appeared, and the page said to try again. Now a new card, a new column or a logged focus entry is sent once more on the latest board, so it is saved on the first click. Moves and edits are still refused when the board changed under them, as before.
  - Not verified: reproduced and checked only in the standalone preview (headless Chromium, 1280 wide, with the daily plan's answer held on purpose) and in the store's unit tests; not in DSH, the desktop app or the Spring build. The add of a column and of a focus entry use the same path but have no browser test of their own.
