- **In the Windows desktop app—or any browser window where tab sharing is blocked—Live class can now capture your computer's playback audio directly through an on-demand hot-installable component, with no virtual sound card and zero idle background process.** Selecting **Computer audio (desktop / online class)** on Windows 10/11 records what your speakers, wired headphones, or Bluetooth headphones are playing without opening a browser share picker or changing your default recording device:
  - **On-demand hot-install and one-click uninstall**: nothing is pre-bundled or running until you click **Install system-audio component**, which builds a ~20 KB read-only helper locally in 1–3 seconds (no download or administrator rights needed), pins its SHA-256 checksum before every launch, and lets you remove it anytime in one click under **Settings › Audio transcription**.
  - **Zero sound-card interference when idle or paused**: the helper reads shared-mode WASAPI loopback without installing virtual audio drivers, opening dummy playback streams, or switching Bluetooth headphones into low-quality hands-free call mode; **Pause**, **End recording**, or closing the window immediately exits the process and releases every audio handle, and plugging or unplugging headphones mid-class switches automatically. Not verified: live hot-swap latency across third-party USB DAC drivers or multi-channel surround gaming headsets inside a packaged desktop DSH client.
