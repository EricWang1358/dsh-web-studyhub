- **Deleting job records, notes, Learning flow records, annotations, PDF conversion records, archived live classes and interactive diagrams is one click with 撤销 (Undo); archiving a job, a source or a deck says so and can be undone.** These now leave at once with a notice 「已删除 …（8 秒内可撤销）· 撤销」 instead of a confirmation: job records in 任务 (Jobs: the header's 删除 and the selection bar; the notice also says which records lose their working files and cannot be continued, that sources, drafts and decks stay, and how many running jobs were skipped), a past day of 为你定制, PDF conversion records and 清空历史 (Clear history, which now forgets exactly the rows you saw), 学习流 (Learning flow) templates and study records, a saved annotation thread (deleted at once by its answers; Undo puts exactly those back, so an answer kept on the same passage meanwhile is never lost), an archived live class and an interactive diagram. The deletion is sent when that notice leaves (its time runs out, you close it, another notice replaces it, you go to another page or library, or the page is hidden or closed); a deletion that fails puts the item back and says why. Started from the keyboard, the notice waits until you close it and 撤销 puts the focus back on the item. 学习笔记 (Notes) gets 删除这篇笔记 (Delete this note) in an open note's ⋯ menu, with the same undo; it also stops a draft being written for that note. Drafts (待发布) still ask once, in one dialog that names the draft as its row does and says what really goes (a new draft's unpublished questions, or only the unpublished changes of a published deck, which stays), what stays, and that a job writing it stops; while its publication check runs it waits instead; cancel is 保留 (Keep). The 待发布 rows on the library home have their own 删除 that opens the same dialog. 归档 (Archive; it was 知道了 / Got it, and 全部归档 / Archive all was 全部知道了) on finished jobs, and archiving a source or a deck, show a notice with 撤销 and, for a source or a deck, 永久删除… (Permanently delete…), which opens the existing permanent-delete dialog; the deck notice says how many open practice runs the archive ended and that 撤销 does not reopen them. Sources and decks keep archive-first: nothing about permanently deleting them changed except the wording (the source dialog now says 永久删除 throughout; the skeleton dialog says 永久删除 too). Not migrated yet (they still ask in a dialog): deleting a skeleton, clearing old-version annotations, deleting all usage records, clearing the companion profile, deleting a board card. Not verified: whether a deletion still inside its 8 seconds reaches the host when the DSH desktop app closes its window (sending on page close is best effort; a page reload in Chromium sent it; if the request is cut off the item simply comes back); a library switched from outside the app during the 8 seconds keeps the item and says so; tried in Chromium with the preview server and a seeded library at 1280, 420 and 320 px, not in the DSH desktop app.
