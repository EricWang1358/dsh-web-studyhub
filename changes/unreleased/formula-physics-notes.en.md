- **Physics formulas draw in cards, the review book and text sources, and the mistakes page draws its formulas too.** In study cards (also 错题与待巩固 and the review book) and in the reader's text and PDF-text sources, `$ F = ma $` (a space inside the dollars on both sides), `5$kg$` (a unit right after a number) and `\begin{equation}…\end{equation}` / `\begin{align}…\end{align}` without dollar signs are read as formulas now; prices with a space after the sign (`$ 5 + $ 3`) and format marks such as `%1$s` stay text. The common `physics` and `siunitx` commands (`\dv{x}{t}`, `\pdv{f}{x}`, `\vb`, `\abs`, `\norm`, `\SI{3}{m/s}` with a plain number and unit, `\unit`, …) draw instead of falling back to their source wherever the cards' renderer is used; a form that cannot be drawn with certainty (`\dv[2]{x}{t}`, `\dv{x}` with one argument, `\SI[…]{…}{…}`, `\SI{1.6e-19}{C}` and other numbers siunitx draws as a power of ten or a ±, `\qty`, `\ev`, `\va`, `\ang`, `\num`) still shows its source. In an opened mistake the prompt line, your answer, the correct answer, the explanation, the pitfall, the variants and the similar questions show their formulas as formulas, a long line no longer pushes the panel wider than the page (the text wraps, a long formula scrolls inside its own box), and on a narrow page the label sits above its value. The generation check and the automatic fix now read formulas with the same scanner as the page, so they no longer wrap a formula the page already draws. Imported Markdown and pasted sources in the reader draw through the same renderer, so they get the commands but keep their own dollar rules.
