- **Physics and math notes draw their formulas, also in 错题与待巩固.** Formulas written as `$ F = ma $` (spaces inside the dollars), `5$kg$` (a unit right after a number) or as `\begin{equation}…\end{equation}` / `\begin{align}…\end{align}` without dollar signs are read as formulas now, and the common `physics` and `siunitx` commands (`\dv`, `\pdv`, `\vb`, `\SI`, `\unit`, `\ang`, …) draw instead of falling back to their source. In an opened mistake the prompt line, your answer, the correct answer, the explanation, the pitfall, the variants and the similar questions show their formulas as formulas, and a long line no longer pushes the panel wider than the page: the text wraps and a long formula scrolls inside its own box. On a narrow page the label sits above its value. Not verified: the DSH desktop app's own Chromium (tried in Chromium with the preview and a seeded library at 1280 and 420 px), a real model's physics notes, and commands outside the list (`\qty` and the rest of siunitx still show their source).
