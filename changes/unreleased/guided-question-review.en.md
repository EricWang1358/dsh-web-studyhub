- **A new question written during AI guided study is now really written and reviewed, and opens on the right to be answered.** In 4.0.0 "prepare a new question" wrote neither: it stored whatever text the main chat passed (or, with none, a placeholder "Option A (correct)") as an approved question, and a wrong deck id put it into your first deck. Now it takes the same path as any added question: one question from the session's material, quotes checked and reviewed independently, then published into the existing deck of that course you chose, and opened in the guided workbench as a round of that one question. If it does not pass, the deck is unchanged and the workbench says why and offers your existing questions. A choice question on the workbench is answered on its options and graded by its answer, no longer only "I've got it"; stems, options and explanations draw formulas and images. Nothing starts when the session does not allow new questions, the deck does not exist or belongs to another course, or the material is outside the session. Not verified: the whole flow in a real DSH main chat with a real model (writing, review, publication and answering were run with fake models); placeholder questions that 4.0.0 already wrote are not removed: check your decks for questions whose topic is "带学新题".
