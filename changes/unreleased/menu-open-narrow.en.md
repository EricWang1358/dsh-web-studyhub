- **A 「更多」 menu no longer shuts by itself the moment it opens on a narrow window.** Opening a menu moved focus to its first item, which could scroll the page a little, and any scroll closed the menu, so on a phone-width window 「更多」 under a question sometimes flashed and vanished. Focus now moves without scrolling, and a scroll closes the menu only when its button actually moves; a resize still closes it. Not verified: in the DSH host's own window; checked in Chromium at 420 and 1280 px.
