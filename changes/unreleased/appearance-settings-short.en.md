- **Settings › Interface is short now.** Language, light or dark, accent colour and interface size are at the top, with 「Restore default appearance」 under them. Fonts, animation, contrast, density and corners moved into 「More appearance options」 (everything is still there, nothing was removed). Moving your look to another computer no longer shows a raw text box: 「Copy appearance settings」 copies it, 「Save as file」 saves it, and 「Import appearance」 opens a small window where you paste it or pick the file. Search (找功能) and 「Take me there」 open the fold and light up the control, also for the ones inside it. Not verified: the copy button was only tried in the test browser, where the clipboard is allowed; if your browser refuses, it says so and offers the file.
