- **At an interface size of 150% or in a very short window, a flipped flashcard keeps its 0–5 grade buttons on screen.** When the card does not fit, the grade row stays at the bottom of the window and the card scrolls behind it, so you can still scroll to the card's last line; in a short window (under 640 px tall after scaling, such as 1440×900 at 150%) each button becomes one line, number and words, and the gap to the next review moves into the button's tooltip. At 100% in usual windows nothing changes. Measured at 150% in 1440×900, 1536×730, 1280×720 and 1280×600: all six buttons can be clicked without scrolling.
