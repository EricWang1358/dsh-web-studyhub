- **At an interface size of 150% or in a very short window, a flipped flashcard keeps its 0–5 grade buttons on screen.** When the card does not fit, the grade row stays at the bottom of the window (or of the reader's This section's questions panel), in that page's or panel's own colour, and the card scrolls behind it, so you can still scroll to the card's last line; when the card fits, the row looks as before. In a short window (under 640 px tall after scaling, such as 1440×900 at 150%) the row is just the six buttons, one line each, number and words: the mastery line and the two group titles are left out (the groups differ by colour, and screen readers still read their names), and the gap to the next review moves into the button's tooltip. At 100% in usual windows nothing changes. Measured at 150% in 1440×900, 1536×730, 1280×720 and 1280×600: all six buttons can be clicked without scrolling; at 420×860 the row takes less than a fifth of the window. Not verified: not looked at inside DSH, the desktop app, Safari or Firefox; questions in a companion page's side panel were not checked; in the English interface the longer button words may wrap to two lines.
