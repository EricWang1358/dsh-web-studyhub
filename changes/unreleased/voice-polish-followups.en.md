- **Four small fixes around answering by voice.**
  - **A tooltip no longer opens just because a program moved focus onto its control.** After a spoken flashcard answer the suggested grade takes the focus, and its "Shortcut 4" card used to open and cover the neighbouring labels on a phone. Every tooltip now opens on hover, on keyboard focus (Tab), on a click or tap on its own control (a 「?」 or 名词解释) and on a long press, not when a program moves focus there after a press elsewhere; the suggested grade also takes focus without a card.
  - **The practice header keeps its height when "Corrections to read" appears.** On a phone it used to wrap onto another line and push the card down. The group's mastery now always sits on its own second line at phone width, and the count is no larger than the text beside it; a flashcard's head has room for the extra line.
  - **One name for each voice button, in Chinese and English.** Practice and the oral mock interview both say "Answer by voice" (the oral mock said "Speak"), and the button beside "Help me understand" says "Ask by voice" (it said "Say your question"). The shortcut help, the feature finder and the oral mock's steps use the same names.
  - **Esc closes the audio setup guide in one press.** With the voice button's shortcut card open under the pointer, the first Esc only closed the card; the card now steps aside when the guide opens. Esc for dialogs above it is unchanged. Not verified: a real microphone and a real transcription service, a screen reader, and real touch devices (checked with a simulated microphone and the fake model at 420 px and 1280 px in Chinese and English; the card behaviour also in a small page with the shared tooltip, including a simulated tap).
