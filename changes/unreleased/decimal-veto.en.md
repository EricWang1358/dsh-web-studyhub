- **A book's chapters no longer vanish because one heading starts with a decimal.** A book that numbers its sections 1—1, 1—2, 2—1 and has a single heading such as “1.5 亿的规模效应” keeps the chapters its own titles name, instead of falling back to a chapter per page. Not verified: the owner's real 513-page book, and the screens that list chapters — only the heading rule and its tests were run here.
