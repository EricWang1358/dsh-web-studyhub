- **A new “Minimal mode” toggle removes visual flourishes, flattens cards, and quiets startup and Today’s board.** Found at the top of Settings › Interface (off by default, saved with your browser appearance and included in appearance export/import and reset), it flattens paper cards to match the active dark or light theme without losing AA contrast, strips stacked-card shadows, ruling lines, and decorative motion, temporarily mutes sound effects and pauses desk soundscapes/lighting without erasing saved presets, and hides the origami/lucky-slip strip, seal stamps, confetti, ink underlines, rolling numbers, and hand-drawn sketch accents while keeping all seal-album records intact. Opening StudyHub stays on the Study library instead of auto-jumping to Board › Today; Board › Today merges Morning/Afternoon lanes into one daily list without the Pomodoro card or right rail (keeping a compact pause/abandon bar if a timer was already running, and preserving underlying AM/PM slot data); and the top bar hides static library/model chips and the idle Connected label while keeping all warnings and inbox alerts. Turning Minimal mode off restores every previous setting and view.
