- **You can now answer a section's questions right beside the text: the learning panel of the reader starts with 本节题目 (This section's questions), and the round is answered there, with the same question, keys and steps as on the practice page.** The panel lists the questions of the section on screen (or, with 本章, of the chapter), at most 30; click one to start the round at it, or 在这里做这 N 道题 to start at the first. Your answers count exactly as on the practice page: the same attempts, the same next review dates, the same daily summary, mistakes list and mastery. The text does not move while you answer, and 回到题目列表 brings the list back with the text where it was. A larger range goes to the practice page (还有 N 道，去练习页做全部), 去练习页继续 takes the round there, and 做这几页的题 has a 在这里做 button for the range chosen there. Under the heading, 错题 n · 追问 n · 批注 n count the section's mistakes, follow-up questions and notes; each opens what already shows them (the 错题 panel, the follow-ups with a way to answer that question here, the notes in the text). On a narrow window the panel slides over the text as before. Opening the learning panel on a wide window no longer shifts what you were reading. Not verified: with a real 500-page book on a slow computer; screen readers.
