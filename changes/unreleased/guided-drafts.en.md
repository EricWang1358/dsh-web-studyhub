- **A half-written answer is no longer lost, and your answer record now tells helped answers from your own.** What you have typed, ticked or filled in saves by itself once you pause. It is saved before you go to the previous or next question, jump to a prerequisite, follow a link or move the question to the right sidebar, and it is back when you return to the question after reloading the page or reopening DSH. Something you cleared stays cleared. Words you typed also survive the main chat adding or removing other questions in the round. If it cannot be saved (the connection dropped, or a save hangs for about 8 seconds), that step does not go ahead and the page says so; your words are still there, and you can click again once you are back online. Saving calls no model, costs no quota and does not get in the way of typing. An answer given after opening the hint, asking in 帮我弄懂, or clicking 请教主会话 in guided study is recorded as helped; one you gave alone is recorded as independent. Flipping a flashcard to grade it does not count as help, because the flip is how a flashcard is answered. A question you get right only on the second try in the same round, after a wrong answer showed the right one, also counts as helped, not as independent, and the check item now reads "Done, not on your own" (非独立完成) instead of naming a hint nobody opened. Until now every answer was wrongly recorded as helped; that is fixed, but existing records are not rewritten: answers from v4.0.0 to v4.1.0 all stay marked as helped, so a guided session that began before this update and continues after it may show fewer independent passes than you earned. Whether the hint or explanation was open is not saved, so they come back closed. Not verified: restoring after a restart inside the DSH host (the test reloads the page in the browser preview), what the right sidebar shows when it takes over a question, phones and screen readers. Options picked on the guided-study page are not saved yet, 请教主会话 being recorded is tested on the real guided page with a stand-in for the host, not walked through inside DSH, and nothing on the page says 已保存 when a save succeeds.
