- **Tapping a word on a touch screen marks it once.** On a touchscreen or with a pen, tapping a word while marking unknown words could mark it and then unmark it straight away, because the click that follows the tap sometimes arrived a moment late (follow-up to #1759). That follow-up click is now ignored however late it comes, and a new touch or a short wait clears the rule. Not verified: not tried on a real phone or tablet, only in a simulated touch browser.
