- **The draft page now checks the draft by knowledge point: 「70 个知识点，17 个还没有题」 (70 knowledge points, 17 without a question), and one action, 「补这些知识点」 (Fill these knowledge points).** The knowledge points are the points of the course outline (总纲) that the draft's materials teach. A question counts for a point when its source quote lands on the text the inventory found for that point; a question whose quote lands on no such text counts for the point that owns its page, as before. Questions in every deck and draft of the course count. Under the line, a fold lists the points without a question (at most 30), the ones others build on first and marked 前置 (prerequisite), each with what it rests on, and says which ones cannot be filled because their text was not found in the draft's materials. 「补这些知识点」 shows its token estimate first and runs as a top-up like the page's others: one round of at most the round limit (30 questions), one question per point (two for a point of 20 or more inventory items), each written from that point's own passage and titled after it, in the way the draft was written (one pass or staged), through the same checks and review. A point whose question the review drops stays on the list. A request for a point the outline no longer has, one that already has a question, one with no text in the draft's materials, or more questions than one round is refused as a whole, with nothing started. Without an outline built from the inventory (no outline, an old one organised by material names, a lost inventory, or materials changed since), one quiet line says why and where to build it; the page never starts an outline build by itself.
  - **The 总纲 page counts the same way.** When the outline was built from the inventory, the question counts of its points and its 未出题 (no questions yet) filter use the same link, so a point that teaches on a page it does not own (no page-based count could ever give it one) now gets its questions, and the page's owner no longer gets them too. Draft questions are also counted for points anchored to a page range, which they were not before. An outline without an inventory counts by pages exactly as before.
