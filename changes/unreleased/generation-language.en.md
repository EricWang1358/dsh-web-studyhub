- **Questions now come out in the question language you chose, also from Chinese material: with English chosen, stems, options, answers, hints and explanations are English.** Before, only the writing step was told the language, so the step that fixes answers and options often kept the source's Chinese and a question came out half Chinese. Now one rule, decided once from your 出题语言 setting, goes to every step that writes or checks a question (plan, blueprint, writing, review, wording repair, extra rounds, a passage added to a deck, 待出题, questions from a live class, case papers, draft repair, variants, rewrites after feedback, 修题 and 出成题), and it outranks the interface language. A question still in the wrong language is fixed in the same run without extra model calls, or replaced, or held back in the draft with the reason "Part of it is not in the question language you chose" instead of being published; a step that keeps writing the wrong language no longer fails the whole part. 中英双语 asks for Chinese first, with the stem also in English and key terms with their English name; any layout with both languages in the stem is accepted (the English in brackets or as a second sentence), and a flashcard front may be just the term. Questions made from an existing deck or card keep its language. Drafts made before this version are not held back for their language. Not verified: with real models, how often a first answer is in the wrong language; the English check allows short Chinese names and quoted source text, so a long quoted passage is not flagged.
