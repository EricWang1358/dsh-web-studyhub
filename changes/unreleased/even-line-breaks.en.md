- **Chinese text no longer leaves a single character alone on its last line.** A paragraph keeps at least two characters on its last line, so the today card reads "…留到下" / "一轮" instead of stranding "轮"; headings and the short description under each page title split into lines of similar length (on a narrow window the create-deck description becomes two even lines instead of a long line and "可以直接导入。"). A browser that does not know these line-breaking rules keeps the old breaks. Not verified: the DSH desktop app on a real machine and other browsers than Chromium; the pages were checked in Chromium at 1440 and 420 pixels wide, and the today card still splits the phrase "下一轮".
