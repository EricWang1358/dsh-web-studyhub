- **Study notes and case exam reports no longer stay stuck when AI drafting or background grading is interrupted.** In Study Notes, you can now click "Stop drafting" while AI is writing a draft, and reopening a note after restarting DSH marks an unfinished draft as interrupted so you can edit, save, or start drafting again right away. In Case Paper exam reports, if background grading fails or did not finish before a restart, the spinner stops, the report shows what happened with a "Submit for grading again" button, and retrying sends your saved answers for the remaining ungraded questions instead of blank answers.
