- **Stopping a live class while the recognizer is reconnecting no longer loses the audio it was holding.** The stop waits a moment for the connection to come back, sends it the audio heard during the outage and keeps that text. If the connection does not come back in time, the class still ends and its record says how many seconds were not transcribed, instead of dropping them without a word. The same count now covers every other way audio was lost to an outage: an outage longer than the 30 seconds StudyHub holds (the oldest audio is let go), and a reconnect that gives up. Not verified: a real recognizer or a real network drop; checked with a stand-in connection only.
