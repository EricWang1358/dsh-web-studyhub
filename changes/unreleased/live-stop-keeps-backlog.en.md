- **Stopping a live class while the recognizer is reconnecting no longer loses the audio it was holding.** The stop waits a moment for the connection to come back, sends it the audio heard during the outage and keeps that text. If the connection does not come back in time, the class still ends and its record says how many seconds at the end were not transcribed, instead of dropping them without a word. Not verified: a real recognizer or a real network drop; checked with a stand-in connection only.
