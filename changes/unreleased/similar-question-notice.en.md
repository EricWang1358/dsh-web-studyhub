- **The draft page points out questions that ask the same thing in other words.** When a draft question closely resembles a question of the same kind in the same draft or in a published deck of the same course, its row starts with "Similar to question 12 of …" (a long deck title is cut short, never the words or the number); open the question to read the other one side by side. It is only a heads-up: both questions stay, and saving and publishing work as before; while a background job is still changing the draft it is not checked again, only once the job is done. A choice question and a fill-in-the-blank on the same point are not flagged.
