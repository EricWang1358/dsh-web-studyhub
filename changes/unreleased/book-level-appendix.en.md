- **A book's own appendix at the end is a chapter of its own now.** In a book that numbers its sections 1—1, 1—2 …, the 附录, 索引 or 参考文献 that come after the last chapter's 本章提要 / 操作题 end block are listed as chapters of the book, and the last chapter ends at that block instead of running to the last page. Not verified: the owner's real 513-page book, and the screens that list chapters — only the heading rule and its tests were run here.
