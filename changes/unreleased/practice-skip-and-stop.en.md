- **Inside a practice round you can skip a question for now, or stop the round with a result.** “Skip for now” in “More” (or K) sets the question aside: it comes back at the end of the round, nothing is recorded for the skip, and its time starts again when you come back. “Next question” now goes to the next question still waiting, so once the set-aside one is answered the next press opens the result (← still steps back to re-read). “Stop here…” (in “More”, at the end of the question column, or in the list behind “N to answer” on a narrow window) asks once, then opens the result page: answered questions count and are scheduled as usual, unanswered ones record nothing, and “Auto-advance” does not carry the stopped round on. Write a note (N), mark as focus (F) and the question's own page (O) now have keys too, listed in the shortcut sheet and the finder. A mock exam is unchanged. Not verified: in the DSH host (checked in the browser preview with sample data only), and with a screen reader.
