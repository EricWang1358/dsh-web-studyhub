- **The PDF-conversion status no longer makes the Settings and audio pages wait several seconds.** The local MinerU check used to start the program (two Python start-ups, about 2 s and 5 s here) every time its answer was a few seconds old. Now the two checks run together, the answer is kept until something that can change it happens (an install, an uninstall, another folder, starting or stopping the service, a conversion), and an old answer is shown at once while a fresh one is read in the background. Starting an import or pressing re-check still reads the real state. On this computer a repeated check now takes about 15 ms instead of 5 to 9 s, and the first check after the app starts about 4 s instead of 7 s. Not verified: the first check after starting the app still waits for the program itself (about 4 s with a real MinerU installed); a computer with no MinerU answers at once.
