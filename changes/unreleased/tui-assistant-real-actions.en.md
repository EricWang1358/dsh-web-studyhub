- **The TUI assistant answers by what it can really do, and no longer says "已完成" when nothing happened.** The assistant can skip a card, move a card to the end of the queue, check grading status and explain a card. A request it cannot fulfil gets a short explanation with the real entry point, and irreversible actions still ask for confirmation. A failed action shows a readable reason and an "[A] 重试" retry entry; "已完成" appears only after a real backend side effect. The "标记错因" chip appears only after a wrong answer and offers six reasons, and undo clears the mark; the wrong-book export shows the reason. Score changes accept only an explicit 0–5. Explain, guided, retell and follow-up chats share one send / abort layer and one prompt core; follow-ups are multi-turn, show source excerpts for questions tied to page ranges, and clear when you switch card or result. Notes saved from explanations get plain titles, and `note.qa.save` no longer overwrites a saved follow-up by default. The term glossary can suggest AI translations behind a confirmation, with manual entry as the fallback. Not verified: behaviour against a live model provider in a real terminal; the tests use fake models.
