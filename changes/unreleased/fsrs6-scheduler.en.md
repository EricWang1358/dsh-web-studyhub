- **Reviews are now scheduled with FSRS-6, and each answer button shows when the question will come back.** FSRS-6 estimates how long you will remember a question from your answer and from the days that really passed since its last review, and brings it back when the chance of still remembering it drops to your target (90% by default). Answering a question before it is due stretches its gap less than before, and answering it again on the same day (your computer's calendar day) no longer stretches it at all; a wrong answer on a question you knew shortens the gap, though not always back to one day. Under each 0–5 button you now see the gap it would give (for example 1 d / 3 d / 8 d on a new question). Your existing questions keep their due dates and history: nothing is lost or rewritten, and each one switches over at its next answer, when its old interval becomes its starting point. In Settings › Import, schedule and backup, Spaced repetition now has two optional numbers, Target recall and Maximum gap, each explained when you hover its icon; the four old SM-2 numbers are gone, so values you had changed there no longer apply. Mastered now also takes at least two right answers in a row (a self-graded 5 still counts as Mastered at once), and with a small maximum gap the Familiar and Mastered lines come down to it, so they stay reachable. The idea and the port come from community user BAXUIE's StudyHub for ZCode, thank you! Not verified: whether the published FSRS-6 default weights predict well for learners with only a few answers per question (StudyHub does not tune them per learner); answering on several days in a row still raises a question to Mastered by the fifth day, which is a separate fix.
