- **Questions you keep failing are marked "Stubborn" in the wrong book, with the rescue actions in the opened row.** A question failed 4 times in all, or with 60% or more of its latest 5 attempts failed, carries a small "Stubborn" mark in both the To review and the Corrected views, told from your answer log alone (no model call). Open its row for four ways out that already existed elsewhere: fix it in the background, add a prerequisite card, send it to the main chat, or suspend it. A collapsed row gains no button; "Stubborn" above the list shows only these questions; a finished round that held any says so in one line with a link to them; and the two numbers can be changed in Settings › Practice. Not verified: not tried in the live app with a real library; the mark, the filter, the result line, the settings and the finder entries were checked in server-rendered tests, and the four actions were not clicked against a real model. The 4 and 60% defaults are untested on real data.
