- **Questions you keep failing are marked "Stubborn" in the wrong book, with the rescue actions in the opened row.** A question failed 4 times in all, or with 3 or more misses among its latest 5 attempts (60% of 5; with fewer than 5 attempts so far it counts the same way), carries a small "Stubborn" mark in both the To review and the Corrected views, told from your answer log alone (no model call; prerequisites passed on the way to another question are not answers and do not count). It clears once the latest 5 attempts are all right, and answering right never makes a question stubborn. Open its row for four ways out that already existed elsewhere: fix it in the background, add a prerequisite card, send it to the main chat (about this one question), or suspend it (with an Undo). A collapsed row gains no button; "Stubborn" above the list shows only these questions; a finished round that held some says so in one line with a link that opens the wrong book on exactly those questions; and the two numbers can be changed in Settings › Practice. Not verified: not tried in the live app with a real library or a real model; the mark, the filter, the result line, the settings, the hand-off text and the finder entries were checked in server-rendered tests, and the notes after each action were checked against the service with a fake model. With more than 1,000 weak questions the page loads the first 1,000, so the chip counts are of the loaded page. The 4 and 60% defaults are untested on real data.
