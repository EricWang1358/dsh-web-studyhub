- **Reorganising the course outline no longer puts your review-book notes under a title they do not match, and the outline can be cut coarser.** After a reorganise, your own text in the review book still follows its pages, but text whose pages now sit mostly in a point about something else waits in the book's Not placed, naming the closest point and how much of its pages are there; every move records that share. When the book holds your own text, the assistant must ask you before it reorganises the outline (the outline page's Reorganise button counts as asking). The assistant can also ask for coarser or finer knowledge points, or a maximum number of them; neighbouring points are then merged under both names, nothing dropped, and later reorganising keeps the same cut. Nothing changes for outlines made without these choices. Not verified: a real course with a rebuilt inventory outline and long notes, or a real model following the new size wording; checked on the test library with a fake model only. The book page still shows only the first moves and still hides the notice once dismissed.
