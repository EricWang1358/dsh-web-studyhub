- **Reorganising the course outline asks before it moves your review-book notes, and holds back the ones no new point fits; the outline can be cut coarser.** After a reorganise, your own text in the review book follows its pages to the new knowledge points; when no new point holds enough of a note's pages, it waits in the book's Not placed, naming the closest point and how much of its pages are there, and the book page says how many points' notes were put there. A note whose pages were cut into parts follows into its biggest part whatever that part is called, so it can still end under a title about something else (for example when only a small slice of a long chapter survives); a note made by an older version for one whole material follows only when the new points are mostly cut from that material, otherwise it waits in Not placed. When the book holds your own text, the assistant must ask you before it reorganises the outline, from the outline or with the review book's reorganise; the buttons on the outline page count as asking. When the review book reorganises the outline by itself (your materials changed, or the outline was the quick one made from the materials' names), it does not ask; the notes then follow their pages the same way. The assistant can also ask for coarser or finer knowledge points (finer only changes outlines grouped from the inventory), or a maximum number of them; neighbouring points of one chapter are then merged under both names, nothing dropped, chapters are never merged (a maximum they cannot reach is said on the task), and later reorganising keeps the same cut. Outlines made without these choices are cut as before, except that a course so large it is merged to stay under 800 parts now names a merged point after both points. Not verified: a real course with a rebuilt inventory outline and long notes, or a real model following the new size wording; checked on the test library with a fake model only. The book page still lists only the first moves and still hides the notice once dismissed.
