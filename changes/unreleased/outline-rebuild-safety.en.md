- **Reorganising the course outline no longer puts your review-book notes under a title they do not match, and the outline can be cut coarser.** After a reorganise, your own text in the review book still follows its pages, also when one material is cut into many points; but text whose pages now sit mostly in a point about something else waits in the book's Not placed, naming the closest point and how much of its pages are there, and the book page says how many points' notes were put there. When the book holds your own text, the assistant must ask you before it reorganises the outline, from the outline or with the review book's reorganise; the buttons on the outline page count as asking. When the review book reorganises the outline by itself (your materials changed, or the outline was the quick one made from the materials' names), it does not ask, but the same check keeps your text off titles it does not match. The assistant can also ask for coarser or finer knowledge points, or a maximum number of them; neighbouring points of one chapter are then merged under both names, nothing dropped, chapters are never merged (a maximum they cannot reach is said on the task), and later reorganising keeps the same cut. Outlines made without these choices are cut as before, except that a course so large it is merged to stay under 800 parts now names a merged point after both points. Not verified: a real course with a rebuilt inventory outline and long notes, or a real model following the new size wording; checked on the test library with a fake model only. The book page still lists only the first moves and still hides the notice once dismissed.
