- **A shorter sidebar, with the rest under “More”.** The sidebar now shows Return to question and the six pages most people use (Study library, Sources, Create deck, Mistakes & weak points, Mock exam, Statistics). Everything else is in “More”, grouped by what it is about: Materials and questions, Practice and review, Exams and statistics, and Tasks, notes and to-dos. Hold an item and drag it up to keep it in the main list (at most six), or use Alt with the up and down arrows. An order you saved before is kept: each page keeps the place you gave it within its new group. Find a feature (Ctrl K) knows where every page went.
- **One dot, and only when something waits for you.** The “Start here” dots are gone. The sidebar shows at most one dot: on Tasks when a task did not finish, else on Return to question when questions are due today, else on To-dos when one is overdue or due today. Hover or focus it to read why. An empty library shows no dot. When the item is folded away, “More” (or the menu button in a narrow window) carries the dot.
- **Language and appearance live in Settings › Interface only.** The sidebar's language and theme switches are gone; the footer now has three items: Find a feature, Help (the tour, keyboard shortcuts and the glossary) and Settings. The language still follows your browser until you choose one.
- **No more row of bare icons in a narrow window.** When the workspace is narrow (a DSH panel of 420 px, say) or you put the sidebar away, a menu button at the left of the top bar opens the navigation as a drawer with every item named. Esc, the close button or a click outside closes it, and the focus goes back to the menu button. The page gains about 60 px of width at 420 px.
