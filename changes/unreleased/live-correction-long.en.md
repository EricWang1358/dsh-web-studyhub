- **Context polish in Live class now copes with very long "sentences" (a fast speaker's 500–2,000 characters in one go), so class notes are no longer lost.** Before, one oversized batch timed out or was cut off and the same batch was sent again every 30 seconds, using up your free quota while nothing got corrected.
  - **Batches follow the text length, not the sentence count**: very long sentences go one at a time, and a request gets more time and reply room when it is bigger. The model now returns only the few words to replace (no whole sentence again), a Chinese translation only for sentences that are not already Chinese, and the running summary only every few batches.
  - **A sentence that cannot be polished no longer blocks the class**: a request that times out or is cut off is retried once with half the text, then one sentence at a time; a sentence that still fails is skipped (its original text is kept), the check moves on, and the line under **Context polish** says so in plain words. **Retry pending correction** tries exactly those sentences again. A network or quota problem is retried at most three times, then waits for your click.
  - **A wrong note or summary no longer cancels that batch's corrections**: it is dropped with a warning and the rest is kept.
  - Not verified: against a real Gemini or DSH model (all checks use fake models, including a replay of a real Chinese class with sentences of 2,419 and 1,990 characters), and the panel does not show the skipped count yet.
