- **The result page lists the round's questions, and each opens a read-only look-back.** “Questions in this round” shows the wrong and unanswered ones first (“Show all” for the rest): the number, right or wrong, whether the hint was used, and the page. Pressing one shows the question, your answer, the right answer, the explanation and its Q&A, without restarting a timer or changing a score; a wrong one also links to the wrong book. An unanswered question shows only itself, so its answer waits for the next practice. “Continue with new questions”, “New questions of this course only” on the home and “Publish and practise” now all take the new-question limit of Settings › Practice (10 of the default 20-question round) instead of always 10; a deck row's start still takes all of that deck's new questions.
