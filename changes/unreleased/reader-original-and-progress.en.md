- **The reader switches between the text and the original PDF in one click, and keeps track of the chapters you have read.** A PDF that has its original file shows a Text | Original PDF switch on the toolbar (in More when the window is narrow): the original opens at the page you were reading, and the material opens in the view you left it in, at the same page. A chapter whose formulas came apart in conversion suggests the original PDF once, or Convert again with MinerU. A chapter scrolled to its end gets a check in Contents with "Read 7/24 chapters"; the check stays in your library (another browser or a restart keeps it) and can be taken back by pressing the check. The linked-questions panel lists this chapter's questions first, with a This chapter / Whole book switch, and [ and ] go to the previous and next chapter. Not verified: with a real lecture PDF whose formulas fell apart (the warning was tuned on made-up text), and on a touch screen.
