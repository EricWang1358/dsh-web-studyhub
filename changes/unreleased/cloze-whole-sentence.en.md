- **Fill-in-the-blank questions keep the whole sentence.** A blank is now written into one whole sentence of the material, with the clause that limits it (although… but, if… then, only if, unless), never only its second half, which can state one case as the rule or say the opposite of the material. Generated questions that start with "but", "therefore" and the like, or keep "although" without its main clause, are sent back for repair; a personalised variant cut that way is dropped. Not verified: tested with a fake model, not checked how often a real model's questions are sent back.
