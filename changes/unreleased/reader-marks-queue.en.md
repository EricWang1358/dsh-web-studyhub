- **Right-click selected text in a material to underline, bold, highlight, queue it for questions, or get an AI explanation.** The marks stay with the material and come back when you reopen it; right-click a mark to remove it (Shift+right-click still opens the browser's own menu, and Copy is the first item). Passages you queue gather under Queued N in the reader's toolbar (under More on a narrow window): remove any, choose 1–3 questions per passage, and Write them all starts one run in the background, so you keep reading while it goes; the questions arrive as one draft, and the passages used leave the list. AI explain keeps a plain, concrete explanation on the words, shown when you point at their wavy line. Not verified: a real model (only the test model wrote questions and explanations), a scanned PDF's text layer, marks after the material is re-imported as a new revision (they stay with the old revision and are not moved), and the inside of DSH.
